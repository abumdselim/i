Where intelligence belongs

Intelligence belongs in the system, not in the pitch.

A model earns its place where the work is repetitive, text-heavy and measurable. That is a narrower set of problems than the market suggests, and it is where we spend our effort: document handling, triage, search, drafting and first-line support.

The rest is ordinary engineering: clean data, a workflow with an exit, and an interface that shows where an answer came from. The model is rarely the difficult part. The system around it is.

Read our approach
Model layer

Chosen against a sample of your real work, evaluated against your edge cases, with a fallback that is tested before launch.

Workflow layer

Volume goes to the machine. Exceptions come to a person, with the context to decide and a record of what was decided.

Interface layer

Answers show their sources. Suggestions stay editable. The screen says plainly what it does not know.
